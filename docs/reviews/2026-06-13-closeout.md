### High

**Score:** **19 of 19 findings fully closed.** M7's residual (the unbounded
`_retrieve_cache`) was found during this closeout and fixed in the same pass —
the cache is now LRU-capped at `RETRIEVE_CACHE_SIZE` (default 256), with a unit
test (`tests/test_pipeline_query.py::test_retrieve_cache_evicts_least_recently_used`).
